# Privacy Policy: Brute Better Best Uploader

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Brute Better Best Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Brute Better Best YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. It isn't offered to anyone else.

## What it accesses
With the channel owner's permission (given through Google sign-in), it uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to that one channel and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and playlists.

## What it stores
The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The tool collects no information about viewers or any other person. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent anywhere except to Google.

## Revoking access
The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## YouTube and Google
This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
